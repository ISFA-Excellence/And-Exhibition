Classes and teaching focus

Every stage needs a different kind of attention.

Classes 1 to 3

Foundation: reading, counting, handwriting and curiosity, in a warm and encouraging setting.

Classes 4 and 5

Building: understanding concepts, practising daily and developing a study routine.

Classes 6 to 8

Strengthening: deeper concepts, problem solving and exam skills for the years ahead.

How we teach

A simple routine that helps children learn with confidence.

Understand first

Every topic starts with a clear explanation, so students know the reason behind the answer.

Practise daily

Regular written practice builds speed, accuracy and handwriting.

Revise and improve

Revision after each topic, with attention to the areas where a child needs more help.
