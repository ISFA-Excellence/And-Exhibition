A coaching institute built around every child

ISFA is a coaching institute in Gadhwa Bazar built around one idea: every child learns better with the right guidance at the right time.

We teach Mathematics, Science, English, CCA and Emerging Technologies & Society to students of Classes 1 to 8 following the BSEB and CBSE syllabus, with clear concepts, regular practice and personal attention led by our Director, Najibullah.

Subjects we teach

Five subjects, taught in step with the school syllabus so students stay confident in class and in exams.

Mathematics

Number sense, problem solving and daily practice that builds speed and accuracy.

Science

Concepts explained through everyday examples, so students understand instead of memorising.

English

Reading, grammar, writing and spoken confidence, step by step.

CCA

Co-curricular activities. Activities that link with school life to build creative and physical skills.

Emerging Technologies & Society

Prepares students to navigate and lead in a rapidly evolving, automated world.
