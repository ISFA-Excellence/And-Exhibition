About ISFA

Learn, Grow, Succeed. Know the people and the purpose behind ISFA.

Education with respect and care

ISFA Excellence and Exhibition is a coaching institute at Gadhwa Bazar, Harpur, Bihar 845454, for students of Classes 1 to 8 in BSEB and CBSE.

We believe a child does best when concepts are clear, practice is regular and someone is paying attention. That is the promise behind our subjects: Mathematics, Science, English, CCA and Emerging Technologies & Society.

Inspired by a Legacy of Care. Dedicated to Your Child’s Future.

Founded in loving memory of a teacher who believed every student can shine, ISFA Excellence and Exhibition blends high-quality academics with the personal mentorship your child deserves.

What drives us

Our mission

To give every child quality education, expert guidance and personal support, so that learning becomes a habit and confidence follows.

Our vision

A community where every student gets the right direction early and grows into a capable, confident adult.

Our promise

Honest teaching, patient support and open communication with every parent.

A message from the Director

ISFA was started with a simple belief: a child who is guided well will dream bigger and reach further. Our aim is to give every student a strong foundation, steady encouragement and the direction to succeed.

Every family that walks through our door is trusting us with something precious. We take that trust seriously, and we promise honest teaching, patient support and open communication with parents.

I invite you to visit us, meet our team and see how we can support your child.

Najibullah, Director
