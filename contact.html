Frequently asked questions

Which classes and boards do you teach?

Classes 1 to 8, for both BSEB and CBSE students.

Which subjects are covered?

Mathematics, Science and English.

How do I apply for admission?

Fill in the admission enquiry form on the Contact page, call or WhatsApp +91 9798926161, or visit us at Gadhwa Bazar.

Where is ISFA located?

Near Dakshin Tola Masjid, Gadhwa Bazar, Bihar. The map on the Contact page shows the area.

How can I share a complaint or suggestion?

Use the Complaint or Feedback tab on the Contact page. A valid mobile number is required so that we can get back to you.

Will someone call me back?

Every enquiry reaches our team by email, and we contact you on the mobile number you provide.
